# Classic guacamole (no stove, about 10 minutes, serves 2 to 4)

Ingredients: 3 ripe avocados, 1 lime, half a small red onion, 1 small tomato, 1 jalapeno (optional), a small handful of
cilantro, salt. Tools: cutting board, chef's knife, spoon, fork or masher, a bowl.

Steps (minutes are from the moment the step starts):

1. (0:00) Check the avocados: a ripe one gives slightly to a gentle squeeze and the stem nub flicks off to show green underneath.
   Rock hard means not today; very soft or brown under the nub means check inside for bruising.
2. (0:30) Finely dice the half onion. Claw grip: fingertips curled under, knuckles guiding the flat of the blade, rock the knife,
   do not saw. Aim for small even pieces, about the size of a grain of rice. Put them in the bowl with a pinch of salt and the
   juice of half the lime; let them sit while you do the rest (this softens the bite).
3. (3:00) If using: halve the jalapeno lengthwise, scrape out the seeds and white ribs with a spoon, and mince it very fine.
   Do not touch your eyes afterwards.
4. (4:00) Dice the tomato small; scoop out and discard the watery seeds first so the guacamole does not go runny. Roughly chop the cilantro.
5. (5:30) Avocados: cut lengthwise around the pit, twist the halves apart. Remove the pit safely: scoop it out with a spoon
   (never stab it with the knife while holding the avocado in your palm). Scoop the flesh into the bowl.
6. (7:00) Mash with a fork, leaving it chunky; stop while you can still see some small pieces of avocado.
7. (8:00) Fold in the tomato, jalapeno and cilantro. Do not over-mix.
8. (9:00) Taste with a chip: more salt if flat, more lime if heavy. Adjust in small amounts and taste again. Done.

Checkpoints to look for and ask about: claw grip and a rocking cut while dicing (say it at once if fingertips are flat or the
caller is sawing); onion pieces small and even; tomato seeds removed; avocado pit removed with a spoon, never a knife held
toward the palm; mash still chunky; seasoned by tasting, not guessing.
