# Garlic butter pasta with greens (one pan plus a pot, about 15 minutes, serves 2)

Ingredients: 200 g pasta (spaghetti or linguine), 3 cloves garlic, 40 g butter, 2 big handfuls of spinach or kale, a lemon,
grated parmesan, salt, pepper, chilli flakes (optional), 1 tablespoon olive oil.

Steps (minutes are from the moment the step starts):

1. (0:00) Fill a large pot with water, add a big pinch of salt, lid on, high heat. Takes about 6 minutes to boil.
2. (0:30) While it heats: peel and thinly slice the garlic. Wash the greens; strip kale leaves off the stems. Zest half the lemon.
3. (6:00) When the water boils hard, add the pasta and stir once. Set 9 minutes for spaghetti (check the packet). Stir once more at 2 minutes.
4. (8:00) Put the pan on medium-low with the olive oil and the butter. When the butter foams, add the garlic. Cook 2 minutes, stirring; it must go pale gold, never brown.
5. (10:00) Add a pinch of chilli flakes if wanted, then the greens with a splash (about half a cup) of the pasta water. Stir until the greens wilt, 2 to 3 minutes. Turn the heat to low.
6. (14:00) Before draining, save one cup of pasta water. Drain the pasta and tip it into the pan with the greens.
7. (14:30) Toss over low heat with the lemon zest, a squeeze of lemon juice and a good handful of parmesan; add pasta water a splash at a time until it looks glossy, not dry.
8. (15:00) Taste: salt, pepper, more lemon if flat. Pan off the heat. Plate, more parmesan on top. Done.

Checkpoints to ask about: is the water at a hard boil before the pasta goes in; is the garlic pale gold (brown means start the
butter again); is there pasta water saved before draining.
